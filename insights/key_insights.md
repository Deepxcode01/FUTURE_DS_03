## 🔍 Key Insights

### 1. Critical Drop-off at MQL → Conversion Stage
- Only **842 out of 8,000 leads** converted (10.53%)
- This is the single biggest bottleneck — 7,158 leads are being lost
- The funnel is essentially 2-stage: Leads → Won/Lost

### 2. Organic Search = High Volume, Needs Better Nurturing
- Organic Search brings the most leads (2,296 — 28.7% of total)
- But raw volume doesn't guarantee high CVR
- These leads need structured email nurturing sequences to move through the funnel

### 3. Referral & Direct Traffic = High Intent
- Both channels show above-average conversion rates
- Users arriving via referral or direct have stronger purchase intent
- These are low-volume but high-quality leads

### 4. Unknown Origin = Attribution Problem
- 1,099 leads (13.7%) have unknown origin
- This means ~13.7% of marketing spend has no trackable source
- UTM parameters and proper campaign tagging must be implemented immediately

### 5. Home Decor & Health/Beauty Dominate Conversions
- Home Decor (105), Health & Beauty (93), Car Accessories (77) are top segments
- Sales team should focus outbound efforts on these categories
- These segments likely have higher seller density and demand

### 6. Online Medium Lead Type = Best Performer
- Online Medium sellers (332 leads) account for the largest share of conversions
- These mid-size online businesses have higher intent and faster decision cycles
- Prioritize this profile in sales outreach and SDR efforts

### 7. Resellers Dominate (70% of Closed Deals)
- 587 resellers vs 242 manufacturers among converted leads
- This suggests the platform appeals more to resellers
- Consider creating tailored onboarding for manufacturers to grow that segment
